Privacy Policy

Moodle-Sync is a personal tool used only by its developer, on their own computer.

Data accessed

  • Google Drive (drive.file): only files and folders the app itself creates, to upload copies of course files.
  • Google Calendar: to create and update events for course assignment deadlines.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored locally in the macOS Keychain. No data is sent to any server other than Google's APIs and the university Moodle site. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
